Latter-day Data

Tools for exploring General Conference talks from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Conference Explorer

Search for any word or phrase and see how often it appears across conferences over time. Drill into a conference to see which talks use it most, then open any talk for a full word analysis and word cloud.
